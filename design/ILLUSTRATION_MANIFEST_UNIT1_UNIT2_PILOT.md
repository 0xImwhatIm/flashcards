- 本 manifest 是產圖與 QA 的中間層；本次使用者選定結果另記於 `design/FINAL_IMAGE_SELECTION_2026-09-21.md`，並已寫入 `index.html`。

- 多角色沿用同一套 mascot grammar，但以大小、姿勢、單一配件或受控輔色區分關係；不做無意義 clone 群像。
- 圖中不放答案單字、字母、數字、logo、浮水印或可讀文字。
- `status` 由生成、QA、使用者選定與 app wiring 分開記錄；63 張選定檔（含 Wave 12 四張修正版）仍是目前 final image set，Wave 13 的 `Above`、`Enough`、`Person`、`People` 是另列的 review-ready 候選。`But` 由使用者明確改選 v1；v2 與四張舊 Unit 1 圖檔仍保留作為歷史。Git commit／publication 仍未執行。

## Shared generation invariant

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: square FlashCards semantic illustration prototype
Input images: Image 1: exact character identity and visual-style reference; do not edit or reproduce the house scene.
Scene/backdrop: warm off-white empty paper background, one thin hand-drawn ground line, minimum props required for meaning, generous upper negative space.
Style/medium: simple children's editorial illustration; thick charcoal-black hand-drawn outline with slight natural wobble; flat matte color fields with very subtle watercolor/colored-pencil tonal variation; no glossy 3D rendering.
Composition/framing: 1:1 square, full subjects visible, main group in the lower half, one clear primary focal point, readable at 160 px.
Lighting/mood: soft diffuse daylight; warm, safe, calm, encouraging.
Color palette: emerald/teal primary mascot, cream paper, muted blue-gray and warm tan environment, at most one restrained mustard or coral accent, charcoal outlines.
Materials/textures: faint paper grain and soft pigment variation; no photoreal texture.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, border, speech bubble, watermark; one clear semantic action or relation; every character and prop must serve the word meaning.
Avoid: semantically unnecessary characters, competing focal points, crowded scenery, hard cast shadows, perfect vector geometry, anime features, realistic anatomy, excessive highlights, neon colors, glossy effects.
```

## QA gates

### Per-image gates

- [ ] Correct word meaning is inferable with the word hidden.
- [ ] Cast count matches the manifest; no semantically unnecessary character.
- [ ] Primary character remains anchor-consistent: bean silhouette, green body, dot eyes, small mouth, rounded limbs, back plates.
- [ ] Relationship / spatial relation reads at 160 px.
- [ ] One focal point, upper negative space, warm paper background and shared ground line.
- [ ] Thick imperfect charcoal outline and flat low-saturation color language.
- [ ] No text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, speech bubble or answer leak.
- [ ] File is 1:1 PNG and stored under `assets/visual-prototypes/pilot-v0.1/`.

### Batch decision

`pilot_accepted` requires all per-image gates plus a cross-image review covering 1-, 2- and 3-character scenes. If a scene fails, revise the prompt or make one targeted image edit; do not silently promote the failed asset to a Unit card.
